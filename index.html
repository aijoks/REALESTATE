How the 3D tours work

Photos in, an explorable building out.

1

Photograph the building

Street-level and angled shots of the facade capture materials, storefronts, window rhythm and the crown.

2

Reconstruct in 3D

The facade geometry is reconstructed into a full model including the sides, rear and roof that photos alone don’t show.

3

Guided 360° tour

A cinematic camera path circles the building with stops at every side; grab the view at any time to explore freely.

4

Click into the floors

Pick an available suite and the camera flies to that floor, highlighting exactly where you’d be in the building.
