About Us

First Pioneer Properties, Inc. is a family owned real estate management company which manages approximately 2.5 million square feet of commercial buildings and over 2,500 acres of land all of which are owned by family related entities. First Pioneer finds properties to acquire through an established national network of commercial real estate brokers, corporate tenants and partners.

First Pioneer looks to buy properties that are not being utilized or operated at their highest and best use. With the family’s access to capital and management experience, the property is acquired and developed with an emphasis on long term ownership.

Our Approach

The family views their real estate investments as generational. The land division of First Pioneer’s business buys raw land at the wholesale level and after installing utilities, roads and other necessary infrastructure, sells the land to individual lot owners on a retail basis.
